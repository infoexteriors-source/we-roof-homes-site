# WeRoof design system

Based on the supplied WeRoof Landing and WeRoof Mobile Landing PDFs, October 2026.

Confident, local, straightforward. Maryland homeowners reading in daylight on a phone or laptop need a light, readable interface, with a dark photographic hero and red reserved for offers and next steps.

Use the native Apple system stack, medium-weight headings, 38px mobile hero, 30px mobile section headings, 27px form headings and 16px body copy. Keep the eagle logo. The palette uses warm white, charcoal, muted gray and WeRoof red. Tokens live in app/brand-kit.css.

Desktop: wide roof photograph with headline and offer at left, inspection form at right, followed by a four-part trust strip. Mobile: compact call header, photographic intro, trust strip, then the inspection form on a light background. Service photos become compact rows. The process is horizontal on desktop and vertical on mobile. Shared forms use native service radios, clear field borders and optional unchecked SMS consent.

Preserve existing service, commercial, pricing, calculator and area links. Retain the roof-system overview, financing terms and factual content. PDF sample ratings, placeholder reviews and legal placeholders are reference-only; do not publish them as facts. Existing live Google reviews remain the source of customer reviews.

Photos use a single rounded lower-right corner. Mobile controls are at least 44px; primary actions are 56px. Forms do not move with scroll. Reduced motion uses a still hero. Do not add external font downloads.
