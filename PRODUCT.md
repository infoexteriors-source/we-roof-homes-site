# WeRoof product context

## Register

brand

## Users

Maryland homeowners comparing roofing and exterior services, often using a phone to understand a leak, replacement, storm concern, or project cost. They need clear next steps and a straightforward way to request an inspection.

## Product Purpose

Make WeRoof feel trustworthy and approachable, explain the available services, and convert qualified interest into a free inspection and estimate. The form is an inquiry, not an appointment confirmation or financing application.

## Brand Personality

Confident, local, straightforward. The approved brief calls this “bold local authority” and describes the desired belief as trusted and affordable. Preserve the supplied eagle identity, red accents, and the user's subsequent request for lighter native Apple-style typography.

## Anti-references

The approved brief rejects luxury minimalism, long cinematic flights, hidden essential copy, artificial empty scroll, and moving form controls. Avoid generic repetition, pressure tactics, fabricated reviews, competitor claims, and unverified certification badges.

## Design Principles

1. Give homeowners an obvious next step without making them finish a presentation.
2. Show and explain the physical work through existing roofing photography and the supplied roof-system reference.
3. Use distinct section rhythms and readable service information, not repeated decorative boxes.
4. Earn trust through clear scope, visible terms, honest product information, and documented business details.
5. Preserve the user-approved two-step form and optional SMS consent; never simulate successful delivery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion and JavaScript-disabled experiences. Essential educational text is server rendered. Mobile does not use pinned roof animation. Test readable contrast, narrow viewports, zoom, field labels and state changes.
