# Higgsfield: roof install scroll video

Goal: one continuous clip (or 4 chained clips) of a roof being assembled layer by layer, scrubbed by scroll on the homepage. Labels are added in code, never in the video.

Rules: locked or single slow orbit camera, no cuts, off-white studio background, no text/logos, 16:9, 24fps.

## Master prompt (single clip)

Cinematic 3D product visualization of a residential gable roof being assembled layer by layer, photoreal architectural render style. A slow, smooth three-quarter aerial camera orbit (about 20 degrees total), locked horizon, no cuts, no shake.

Sequence, each layer arriving with a satisfying, precise motion, floating down from above and settling into place with a subtle soft bounce:
1. Bare wooden roof trusses on a clean house frame.
2. Plywood/OSB decking panels slide in and snap across the trusses.
3. Metal drip edge wraps the eaves and rakes.
4. Dark self-adhered ice and water barrier rolls out along the eaves and valleys.
5. Light gray synthetic underlayment unrolls across the deck in overlapping rows.
6. Starter strip shingles line the bottom edge.
7. Charcoal architectural asphalt shingles cascade upward course by course, from eave to ridge, in a rippling wave.
8. Step flashing and pipe boot flashing around a chimney and vent pipe.
9. Ridge vent slides along the peak.
10. Hip and ridge cap shingles lay across the ridge one by one.
11. Aluminum seamless gutters and downspouts glide in and attach under the eaves.

Final beat: the completed roof glints as warm golden-hour sunlight sweeps across it.

Lighting: soft studio key light plus warm golden-hour rim light, gentle ambient occlusion, crisp material detail (wood grain, shingle granules, brushed metal). Background: seamless off-white studio gradient. Clean, premium, satisfying, engineered feel. 16:9, high detail, 24fps.

## Negative prompt

text, labels, logos, watermark, people, workers, hands, tools, camera cuts, jump cuts, shaky camera, fast zoom, motion blur smear, morphing geometry, flickering, warped shingles, melting, extra chimneys, cartoon style, low poly, busy background, sky clouds changing

## 4-clip chain (last frame of each = start frame of next)

1. Structure: bare wooden roof trusses on a house frame; OSB decking panels float down and snap into place across the trusses; metal drip edge wraps the eaves. Slow locked three-quarter aerial view, off-white studio background, photoreal render, no text.
2. Protection: continuing from the decked roof: dark ice and water barrier rolls along the eaves; light gray synthetic underlayment unrolls across the deck in overlapping rows; starter strip shingles align at the bottom edge. Same camera and lighting, no text.
3. Shingles: continuing from the underlaid roof: charcoal architectural asphalt shingles cascade upward course by course from eave to ridge in a smooth rippling wave; step flashing and pipe boot flashing settle around the chimney and vent pipe. Same camera and lighting, no text.
4. Finish: continuing from the shingled roof: a ridge vent slides along the peak; ridge cap shingles lay one by one along the ridge; seamless aluminum gutters and downspouts glide in under the eaves. Camera eases slightly outward as warm golden-hour light sweeps across the finished roof. No text.
